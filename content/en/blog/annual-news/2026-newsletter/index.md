# 2025 Training Deliveries

It was a busy and rewarding year. We delivered a total of **20 trainings, amounting to over 4100 student hours**. This growth reflects the increasing demand for deep technical skills in the cloud native ecosystem. Our curriculum covered:

- Kubernetes (Administrator, Developer, CKAD Preparation)
- Advanced Kubernetes (Security, Policy Management with OPA/Kyverno)
- Infrastructure as Code (Terraform &; OpenTofu, from Intro to Advanced)
- Configuration Management with Ansible

# Looking Forward to 2026

This year is all about refinement and enhancement. We're taking the lessons from hundreds of training sessions and investing in three key areas to provide an unparalleled learning journey for every student.

New, More Extensive Lab Hosting Environments

A great training experience requires a flawless lab environment - in 2025 we experimented with several on-premise, hosted bare metal and classic cloud environments.  In 2026 we will continue to evolve those solutions for the best trainee experience, adapted to different styles of delivery.

### New Training Tools and Interactive Ideas

Virtual training doesn't have to be a passive experience. We are integrating a new suite of interactive tools to foster collaboration and engagement. This includes the use of collaborative digital whiteboards for architecture brainstorming, real-time polls and quizzes to reinforce key concepts, and shared terminal sessions for pair-programming-style problem-solving. Our goal is to make every virtual session as dynamic and engaging as an in-person workshop.

### New Strategic Partnerships

Several new training partners came on board in 2025 - with whom I look forward to working with in 2026.
One of those partners - LearnQuest - gave me the opportunity to interview to be an official HashiCorp Instructor.  After 6 years of Terraform training delivery it is nice to get that recognition.
